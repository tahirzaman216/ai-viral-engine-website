# Privacy Policy — AI Viral Engine

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

AI Viral Engine is a content creation and social media publishing application that generates short AI-related videos and helps publish them to connected social media accounts.

## Information We Access

With account authorization, AI Viral Engine may access information necessary to connect to TikTok and publish videos, including account identifiers, authorization tokens, and posting permissions.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, upload videos, publish authorized content, and monitor publishing results.

## Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials are stored locally in the application environment and are not intentionally published in public repositories. Access is limited to the application operator.

## Data Sharing

AI Viral Engine sends video content and necessary authorization information to connected social media platforms to perform requested publishing operations. It does not sell personal information.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Authentication data is retained while the account remains connected or until it is removed by the application operator. Users may request disconnection or deletion through the contact method listed below.
