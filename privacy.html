AI Viral Engine — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy describes how AI Viral Engine handles information when you connect a social-media account and use its video creation and publishing features.

Information used

With your authorization, the application may access basic profile details, account identifiers, OAuth access and refresh tokens, video files, upload identifiers, and publication status needed to operate connected-platform features.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and transfer videos, request publication when enabled, check publishing status, and prevent accidental duplicate uploads.

Storage and security

In the current desktop workflow, authorization tokens and publishing records may be stored locally on the device running AI Viral Engine. These files should be protected from unauthorized access and must not be committed to public source-code repositories.

Sharing with platforms

When you choose to upload or publish, relevant video content and publishing metadata are transmitted to the selected platform, such as TikTok, under that platform's own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local authorization and publishing records remain until removed by the operator or as required for the application's operation. You may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings and request deletion of locally held information using the contact method on the AI Viral Engine website. Revocation may not automatically delete existing local files or previously published videos.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version and effective date here.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the contact information provided on the AI Viral Engine website.
